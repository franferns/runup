## Full description

Find your path to Avengers: Doomsday — without plot spoilers.

Runup is an unofficial, spoiler-safe catch-up guide for the Disney+ Official 15 homework list. Place yourself on the strand, see what to watch tonight, and track your remaining queue before the December 2026 horizon.

**On Google TV**
- Pair with the Runup web app to sync your watch progress
- Browse your remaining titles in spoiler-safe watch order
- Open tonight's pick and mark titles as watched from the couch
- See days left until the Doomsday horizon

**Spoiler-safe by design**
- Blurbs explain why each title matters — never what happens
- Choose a starting lane that matches what you have already seen
- Optional budget trim when you only have a few hours left

**Unofficial fan tool**
Runup is not affiliated with Marvel Entertainment or The Walt Disney Company. It is a free companion for fans catching up before Avengers: Doomsday.

Pairing and progress sync require the Runup web app at before-doomsday.com.
